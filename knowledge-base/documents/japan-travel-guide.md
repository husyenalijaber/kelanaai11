# Japan Travel Guide

## Overview
Japan is an archipelago in East Asia consisting of four main islands: Honshu, Hokkaido, Kyushu, and Shikoku. It is renowned for its blend of ancient traditions and cutting-edge modernity.

## Best Time to Visit
- **Spring (March–May)**: Cherry blossom (sakura) season, mild temperatures 10–18°C. Peak tourist season.
- **Autumn (September–November)**: Fall foliage (koyo), comfortable temperatures 15–25°C. Second most popular season.
- **Summer (June–August)**: Hot and humid, 25–35°C. Festivals like Obon and fireworks events.
- **Winter (December–February)**: Snow in northern regions. Ideal for skiing in Hokkaido and onsen.

## Top Destinations
### Tokyo
- Shibuya Crossing — world's busiest pedestrian crossing
- Senso-ji Temple in Asakusa — Tokyo's oldest temple (645 AD)
- Shinjuku — nightlife, shopping, and the famous Golden Gai bar district
- Akihabara — electronics and anime culture hub
- teamLab Planets — immersive digital art museum

### Kyoto
- Fushimi Inari Taisha — thousands of vermilion torii gates
- Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion) — Zen Buddhist temple covered in gold leaf
- Arashiyama Bamboo Grove — iconic bamboo forest
- Gion district — traditional geisha neighborhood
- Nishiki Market — "Kyoto's Kitchen" with 400-year history

### Osaka
- Dotonbori — neon-lit entertainment district
- Osaka Castle — 16th-century landmark with panoramic views
- Kuromon Ichiba Market — 170-year-old public market
- Universal Studios Japan
- Shinsekai — retro 1920s-themed entertainment district

### Hiroshima & Miyajima
- Peace Memorial Park and Museum
- Atomic Bomb Dome (UNESCO World Heritage Site)
- Itsukushima Shrine — floating torii gate on Miyajima Island

## Transportation
- **JR Pass**: Unlimited travel on JR trains including Shinkansen. 7-day pass ~$280, 14-day ~$450, 21-day ~$580.
- **IC Cards** (Suica/Pasmo): Rechargeable card for subways, buses, and convenience stores.
- **Shinkansen**: Tokyo to Kyoto ~2h15m, Tokyo to Osaka ~2h30m, Tokyo to Hiroshima ~4h.
- **Tokyo Metro Day Pass**: $5/day for unlimited subway rides.

## Local Cuisine
- **Ramen**: Regional styles — Sapporo (miso), Hakata (tonkotsu), Tokyo (shoyu)
- **Sushi & Sashimi**: Tsukiji Outer Market (Tokyo) for fresh seafood
- **Takoyaki**: Octopus balls, Osaka specialty ($3–5)
- **Tempura**: Deep-fried seafood and vegetables
- **Wagyu Beef**: Premium Japanese beef, Kobe and Matsusaka varieties
- **Kaiseki**: Traditional multi-course Japanese haute cuisine
- **Onigiri**: Rice balls from convenience stores ($1–2), perfect budget meal

## Cultural Tips
- Remove shoes when entering homes, traditional restaurants, and some temples
- Bow as a greeting — deeper bow = more respect
- No tipping — considered rude in Japan
- Keep voices low on public transport
- Cash is still king — many small restaurants and shrines don't accept cards
- Tattoos may restrict access to onsen (hot springs)

## Visa Information
- Citizens of 68 countries can enter visa-free for 90 days (including USA, EU, Australia)
- Indonesia requires a tourist visa — apply at Japanese Embassy, processing 5–7 days
- Required documents: valid passport, return ticket, proof of accommodation, bank statement

## Useful Japanese Phrases
- Arigatou gozaimasu — Thank you very much
- Sumimasen — Excuse me / I'm sorry
- Eigo ga hanasemasu ka? — Do you speak English?
- Ikura desu ka? — How much does it cost?
- Doko desu ka? — Where is it?
- Oishii! — Delicious!
