# Budget Travel Tips for Southeast Asia & East Asia

## Introduction
Traveling on a budget in Asia is very achievable. With smart planning, you can experience incredible destinations for $30–80 per day including accommodation, food, transport, and activities.

## Accommodation Strategies

### Hostels
- Dorm beds: $8–20/night in Southeast Asia, $15–35 in Japan/South Korea
- Private rooms in hostels: $20–40/night
- Best booking platforms: Hostelworld, Booking.com, Airbnb
- Tips: Book well in advance for popular dates; read reviews carefully

### Guesthouses & Homestays
- Family-run guesthouses often cheaper than hotels with more character
- Bali guesthouses: $15–30/night with breakfast
- Thailand guesthouses: $10–25/night
- Japan business hotels (APA, Toyoko Inn): $50–80/night — cheapest comfortable option

### Alternatives
- Couchsurfing: Free accommodation with locals (requires trust-building)
- Work exchanges (Workaway, HelpX): Work 4–5 hours/day for free board and lodging
- Temple stays in Japan/Korea: $30–60/night including meals and meditation

## Food Budget Strategies

### Street Food First
- Indonesia: Warung meals $1–3, Nasi goreng $1–2, Bakso $0.80–1.50
- Thailand: Pad Thai $1–2, Som Tam $1, Mango sticky rice $1.50
- Japan: Convenience store onigiri $1–2, Ramen $7–12, Soba $6–10
- Vietnam: Pho $1.50–3, Banh Mi $1–2, Bun Bo Hue $2–3

### Supermarkets & Convenience Stores
- Japan's 7-Eleven, Lawson, FamilyMart have excellent food at great prices
- Ready meals, fresh salads, hot foods available 24/7
- Average meal: $3–6 from Japanese convenience stores

### Free Breakfast Opportunities
- Many hostels include simple breakfast
- Hotels in Southeast Asia often include rice porridge or toast
- Airbnb hosts sometimes offer breakfast

## Transport on a Budget

### Flights
- **Book 2–3 months ahead** for best prices
- Use Skyscanner, Google Flights for comparison
- Low-cost carriers: AirAsia, Lion Air, Jetstar, Scoot, VietJet
- Be flexible with dates — midweek flights 20–40% cheaper
- Consider nearby airports: fly to Osaka instead of Tokyo to save $100–200

### Overnight Transport
- Overnight buses and trains save one night's accommodation
- Thailand: Bangkok–Chiang Mai overnight train $15–30
- Vietnam: Hanoi–Ho Chi Minh overnight sleeper train $25–50
- Indonesia: Java overnight trains $10–30

### Local Transport
- Apps: Gojek (Indonesia), Grab (Southeast Asia), DiDi (China)
- Always agree on price before getting in non-metered taxis
- Walk when possible — best way to discover neighborhoods
- Rent bicycle in cities: $5–10/day
- Japan: IC card (Suica/Pasmo) for seamless transit

## Free & Cheap Activities

### Free Activities
- Public parks, beaches, markets, and temples (many free)
- Hiking trails: Mount Batur (Bali), various national parks
- Watching sunset at scenic viewpoints
- Street food tours of local neighborhoods
- Beach time anywhere in Southeast Asia

### Cheap Activities ($5–20)
- Snorkeling day trips in Bali/Thailand: $15–25
- Temple entrance fees: $5–15 most places
- Cooking classes: $20–40 (includes meal)
- Cycling tours: $15–25
- Yoga class in Bali: $8–15

### When to Splurge
- Iconic experiences worth the cost:
  - Borobudur sunrise tour ($25–40)
  - Komodo Island tour ($60–100)
  - JR Pass in Japan if taking 3+ Shinkansen rides
  - Scuba diving in Raja Ampat ($80–120/dive)

## Money Management

### Currency Tips
- Always carry some local cash — many places don't accept cards
- Use ATMs at banks, avoid airport money changers (poor rates)
- Notify bank before travel to avoid card blocks
- Charles Schwab and Wise debit cards offer fee-free ATM withdrawals
- Currency exchange: avoid hotels; use local money changers in tourist areas

## Travel Insurance
- **Always buy travel insurance** — medical evacuation from Southeast Asia can cost $50,000+
- Recommended: World Nomads, SafetyWing, AXA
- SafetyWing: ~$42/month, covers most travel emergencies
- Check policy covers adventure activities (diving, trekking) if planned

## Packing Light = Save Money
- Carry-on only avoids checked baggage fees ($20–50 per flight on budget airlines)
- Pack 5–7 days of clothes — laundry services available everywhere ($1–3/kg)
- Bring universal power adapter
- Download offline maps (Maps.me, Google Maps offline) to save data costs
- Portable power bank essential for long travel days
